Our Professional Fee Schedule
TENfold Wealth Accountants
Transparent, fixed-price guidance for property investors, short-term rental operators, business owners and SMSF trustees. Every engagement begins with a clear scope and a written estimate — no surprises at billing time.
This Professional Fee Schedule forms part of our Engagement Letter (APES305) as required by Chartered Accountants Australia & New Zealand and CPA Australia. Where hourly rates are quoted, time is billed in 10 minute increments and you will be charged for time spent (not outcomes). Out-of-pocket expenses are charged on a cost basis. Fee estimates current as at June 2026 and are excluding GST, state stamp duty (if applicable), payment surcharges, and subject to change without notice. Invoices are due and payable upon issue.
