What Affects Removalist Costs in Australia
Home size
A 1-bedroom unit in Australia takes 2–3 hours; a 4-bedroom house takes 6–10+ hours. Larger homes need more movers and bigger trucks, increasing hourly costs.
Distance
Local Australia moves (under 50km) are charged hourly. Interstate moves are usually quoted as a fixed price based on volume and distance.
Access difficulty
Stairs, narrow hallways, long driveways, and lack of parking at your property all add time and cost. High-rise buildings with lift access restrictions are particularly slow.
Day & time
Moving on a weekday is usually cheaper than a weekend in Australia. End of month is the busiest (and most expensive) time — book midweek, mid-month if possible.
Packing services
Professional packing for a 3-bedroom Australian house costs $300–$800. This includes boxes, bubble wrap, and tape. DIY packing saves money but takes days.
Special items
Pianos, pool tables, large safes, and artworks require specialist handling and equipment — have them quoted separately.
How to Save Money on Removalist Cost in Australia
1
Get quotes from licensed Australian removalists — compare hourly rates AND the estimated total
2
Book midweek and mid-month for the best rates — end of month weekends are peak demand
3
Declutter before moving — fewer items means fewer hours and a cheaper move in Australia
4
Ask about insurance — basic cover is usually included but comprehensive transit insurance is usually extra
5
Pack a box of essentials (kettle, phone charger, toiletries, bedding) to keep with you on moving day
6
Check if your removalist charges travel time from their depot to your home — some do, some don't
Frequently Asked Questions — Removalist Cost in Australia
How much does a removalist cost per hour in Australia?
A removalist in Australia costs $120–$220 per hour for 2 movers and a truck. 3 movers cost $160–$250/hr. Most local moves take 3–8 hours depending on home size.
How much does it cost to move a 3-bedroom house in Australia?
Moving a 3-bedroom house locally in Australia costs $800–$2,000. This includes 2–3 movers, a truck, and typically 4–6 hours of work.
How much does an interstate move cost from Australia?
An interstate move from Australia (e.g. to Sydney or Brisbane) costs $3,000–$8,000 for a 3-bedroom house. Price depends on volume, distance, and whether you choose shared or dedicated transport.
How far in advance should I book a removalist in Australia?
Book 2–4 weeks ahead for local moves, 4–6 weeks for interstate. End-of-month dates in Australia book out fastest — secure your date early.
Do removalists pack for you?
Yes — most Australian removalists offer a packing service for $300–$800 (3-bedroom house). You can also book partial packing for fragile or hard-to-pack items only.
What's not covered by removalist insurance?
Standard transit insurance covers damage during the move. It usually doesn't cover items you packed yourself, pre-existing damage, or high-value items unless declared. Ask your removalist for their specific terms.
