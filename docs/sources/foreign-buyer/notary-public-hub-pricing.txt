Ask a question
Fees follow the NSW Society of Notaries guide under the Public Notaries Act 1997. Disbursements (ASIC searches, postage, courier, official DFAT fees) are extra. Complex or multi-document matters may be charged hourly. Submit and pay online through this portal to receive 15% off the guide notarial rates below. Pay by invoice is at the full guide rate.

How much does a notary public cost in NSW?
Wondering how much you pay a notary public? Our fees follow the Society of Notaries NSW guide under the Public Notaries Act 1997. Rates differ for certified copies, witnessed signatures, and apostille applications.
We quote your matter before you pay. Disbursements such as DFAT, embassy, and postage fees are extra.
