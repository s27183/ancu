Having foreign-language documents translated by a NAATI-certified translator is crucial when you need to present them in Australia. Australian authorities, including the Department of Home Affairs, courts and universities, generally require certified translations of documents that aren’t in English, so the translations meet their legal and immigration requirements.
We provide Vietnamese translation and interpreting services through NAATI-certified translators and interpreters to ensure you get accurate results. When we get requests for translation, we review all the requirements before work begins.
Each certified translation carries the translator’s NAATI stamp, signature, and practitioner number, so you can check their certification status. We keep everything transparent for customers using our translation services .

A birth certificate, academic document, legal paper, business document, and website do not require the same translation approach. We consider how you intend to use the translation and assign the work accordingly.
We Handle Both Personal and Business Content

Formatting Matters, Too

Frequently Asked Questions
1. How long will it take to get a NAATI certified Vietnamese translation?
Certified Vietnamese translation of standard documents such as birth, marriage, divorce, diploma certificates, and driver licences takes 72 hours. If you need it sooner, you can choose our express (24 hours) or urgent (12 hours) delivery options.
2. Are your Vietnamese translators certified?
Yes. Certified Vietnamese translations are completed by NAATI-certified translators, and each document is stamped and signed by the translator.
3. Are your translations 100% accurate?
We ensure that every translation is accurate. Every document goes through a proofreading process before delivery. We also share a draft with you to review before we finalise the translation.
4. Can you translate Vietnamese documents with both Vietnamese and English text?
Yes. We review and translate mixed-language documents. A proper review is required so the Vietnamese content and existing English information are handled appropriately.
5. Can you translate Vietnamese business and marketing content for Australian customers?
Yes. Vietnamese websites, product descriptions, advertisements, social media content, customer communications and other business material can be translated or localised with the intended Australian audience in mind.
6. Do I need a NAATI-certified Vietnamese translation for an Australian visa or immigration application?
If you are submitting a Vietnamese document with an Australian visa or immigration application, you will generally need a certified English translation. Our NAATI-certified translations are accepted by the Department of Home Affairs. Requirements can vary by application, so check the current requirements for your specific visa.
What is a page?
1 Page = 250 words maximum (including numbers) on a single sheet. If your document exceeds 250 words, add an additional page for each extra 250 words.
When placing your order, please choose an accurate number of pages. Our project managers may contact you to revise your quote if your estimate is not accurate.
Guidelines for Common Documents:
Most common documents (e.g., driver’s licenses, birth certificates) typically require only 1 page. For lengthy or multi-page documents, please select 2 or more pages.
