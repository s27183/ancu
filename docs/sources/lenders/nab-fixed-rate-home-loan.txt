About our fixed rate home loans
Get more certainty with our fixed rate home loans. If you choose a fixed rate mortgage with NAB, you can secure an interest rate for a set period between one to five years, which gives you certainty that your minimum repayments stay the same for the fixed term.
You can also lock in a fixed interest rate upfront to protect yourself from any rate increases while your home loan application is assessed and prior to settlement. Fees and eligibility criteria apply. Read more about Rate Lock .

The benefits of a fixed rate home loan
Make the most of your NAB home loan with these great features and benefits.
Certainty of repayments
Lock in a great rate and know that your minimum repayments won’t change during the fixed term.
Flexible repayment options
Choose your own repayment schedule – weekly, fortnightly or monthly.
Split your loan
Get the best of both worlds and split your loan into variable and fixed portions.
Pay your loan off sooner
Make extra repayments up to $20,000 within the fixed rate term without paying any fees.
Control from your pocket
Stay on top of your home loan through the NAB app . You can track your home loan progress, estimate your property equity and more.
Lock in your rate upfront
With Rate Lock , you can enjoy the protection from any interest rate increases for 90 days by paying a Rate Lock fee, while your home loan application is assessed.
