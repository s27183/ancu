FAQs
What is a Non-resident Home Loan?
A non-resident home loan is a Full Doc or Alt Doc home loan, which is available to borrowers who are non-residents of Australia or have non-Australian sourced income.
What kind of property can non-residents buy in Australia?
The Foreign Investment Review Board (FIRB) restricts the type of property that non-residents can purchase in Australia. Non-residents are restricted to buying either new residential properties, established dwellings for redevelopment or vacant blocks of land for development. For more information,
click here
.
What are the income currencies accepted in order to apply for a non-resident home loan?
At Brighten, we accept over 24 income currencies, most notably: Australia, New Zealand, China, Hong Kong, Singapore, United Kingdom, United States of America and Vietnam. To see a full list of our accepted currencies, view our
product guide
.
Do non-residents need approval from the Australian Government to get a mortgage?
Yes. The authority on non-resident property purchases in Australia is the
Foreign Investment Review Board
(FIRB). Any non-resident wanting to buy a residential house, apartment or block of land in Australia must satisfy the rules laid out by the FIRB.
Do you offer interest only home loans?
Yes, we do, up to a maximum of five years. The loan will then revert to Principal and Interest repayments for the remaining term.
What is the minimum and maximum home loan size?
The minimum home loan amount is $150,000; and the maximum is $15,000,000.
How much can I borrow?
The maximum amount you can borrow for a home loan depends on various factors, such as your income, liabilities, credit history, and the Loan to Value Ratio (LVR).
