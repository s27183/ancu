The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed over
$820 million to new projects and over $530
million to existing projects in the
Infrastructure Investment Program (IIP),
supporting the delivery and progression of
important upgrades within Queensland.

New Projects
At the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $822.5 million towards two
new projects in Queensland.

Bruce Highway - Gateway Motorway to Dohles Rocks Road (Stage 2)
The Australian Government has committed $812.5 million to upgrade the Bruce
Highway from the Gateway Motorway to Dohles Rock Road. The project will
construct new collector-distributor roads and bridges across the Pine River. This
commitment builds on previous investments made by the Australian Government to
increase capacity and traffic flow on the Gateway Motorway and Bruce Highways and will address a
key pinch point ahead of the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

M1 Pacific Motorway - Exit 45 (North) Ormeau
The Australian Government has committed $10 million to the project, which will
include design and preconstruction activities to improve the overpass at Exit 45. This
investment will build on previous investments made by the Australian Government to
upgrade the southbound off-ramp at Exit 45 on the M1 Pacific Motorway.

Projects Receiving Additional Funding
At the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $534.2 million to support
the delivery of six existing projects in Queensland.

Bruce Highway – Tiaro Bypass
This project will construct a new four lane 9.6-kilometre bypass of the Bruce
Highway at Tiaro, south of Maryborough. The Australian Government has committed
a further $251.2 million towards the project, bringing its total commitment to $520
million. This additional funding will address higher design standards and costs and
to support completion ahead of the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

Cairns Western Arterial Road Duplication
This project will duplicate the Cairns Western Arterial Road between the Redlynch
Connector Road and the Captain Cook Highway. The Australian Government has
committed an additional $166.3 million towards the project, bringing its total
commitment to $382.3 million. This additional funding will support the staged
delivery of the project, including the construction of Section 1 from Lake Placid Road to
Captain Cook Highway ahead of the Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games.

Boundary Road Level Crossing, Coopers Plains
This project will construct a new road-over-rail overpass and remove the existing
level crossing at Boundary Road in Coopers Plains. This will improve safety and
traffic flow for road and rail users in the area. The Australian Government has
committed an additional $99.7 million towards the project, bringing its total
commitment to $279.2 million. This additional funding will support progression of the
project by increasing the Australian Government’s funding share relative to Queensland’s.

Bowen Basin Service Link – Walkerston Bypass
This project delivered a new, two-lane 10.4-kilometre bypass connecting the Peak
Downs Highway to the Mackay Ring Road (Stage 1). The Australian Government
has committed an additional $13 million towards the project, bringing its total
commitment to $194.9 million. This additional funding will finalise the project
following practical completion in September 2025.

Bruce Highway – Caboolture-Bribie Island Road to Steve Irwin Way
This project delivered 11 kilometres of upgrades and constructed an additional lane
in each direction on the Bruce Highway between Caboolture-Bribie Island Road and
Steve Irwin Way. The Australian Government has committed an additional
$2 million towards the project, bringing its total commitment to $328.7 million. This
additional funding will finalise the project following practical completion in
July 2024.

Glass House Mountains Road (Steve Irwin Way) and Caloundra Street
Intersection Upgrade
This project delivered an upgrade to the intersection of Glass House Mountains
Road (Steve Irwin Way) and Caloundra Street at Landsborough. This will enable
improved traffic movement and connectivity for road users in the Landsborough
area. The Australian Government has committed an additional $2 million towards the
project, bringing its total commitment to $5.8 million. This additional funding will finalise the project
following practical completion in December 2025.
