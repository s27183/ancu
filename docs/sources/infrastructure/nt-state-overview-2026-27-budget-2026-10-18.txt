The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed over
$26 million to new and existing projects in
the Infrastructure Investment Program
(IIP), supporting the delivery and
progression of important upgrades within
the Northern Territory.

Northern Territory

This means that as at the 2026-27 Budget,
the Government’s total commitment to the
Northern Territory under the IIP, over the
next 10 years, is $2.8 billion, including
$2.4 billion towards major road and rail
projects and over $340 million for smaller
projects and programs such as the Roads
to Recovery, Safer Local Roads and
Infrastructure, and Black Spot programs.
The Government recognises that
infrastructure delivery continues to be
affected by uncertain global conditions,
including market volatility, supply chain
pressures and higher construction costs.
We will continue working with the Northern
Territory to deliver projects that will benefit
Australians into the future.

New Projects
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $24 million to one
new project in the Northern Territory.

Bagot Road Safety and Capacity Upgrades
The Australian Government has committed $24 million to the project, which will
upgrade the two intersections of Bagot Road with Fitzer Drive, Totem Road and
Osgood Drive to improve traffic flow, reduce congestion and enhance safety.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2025–26 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $2.5 million to
support the delivery of one existing project.

Stuart Highway - Schwarz Crescent Intersection Upgrade
This project will upgrade the intersection between Smith Street and the Stuart
Highway, as well as better connecting Schwarz Crescent and Anzac Hill Road with
the Stuart Highway, for improved road safety and pedestrian access.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $2.5 million towards the
project. This additional funding will address cost pressures resulting from higher
construction costs and extended land acquisitions processes. The total Australian Government
commitment towards the project is now $12.1 million.
