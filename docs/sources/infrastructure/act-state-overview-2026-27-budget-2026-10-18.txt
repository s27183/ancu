The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed over
$54 million to new and existing projects in
the Infrastructure Investment Program
(IIP), supporting the delivery and
progression of important upgrades within
the Australian Capital Territory.

Australian Capital Territory

This means that as at the 2026-27 Budget,
the Government’s total commitment to the
Australian Capital Territory under the IIP,
over the next 10 years, is $646 million,
including $477 million towards major road
and rail projects and $169 million for
smaller projects and programs such as the
Roads to Recovery, Safer Local Roads
and Infrastructure, and Black Spot
programs.
The Government recognises that
infrastructure delivery continues to be
affected by uncertain global conditions,
including market volatility, supply chain
pressures and higher construction costs.
We will continue working with the
Australian Capital Territory to deliver
projects that will benefit Australians into
the future.

new project in the Australian Capital Territory.

Drake Brockman Drive Duplication
The Australian Government has committed $50 million to the project, which will
deliver the staged duplication of Drake Brockman Drive.

Sydney to Canberra Rail Corridor Upgrade
In addition, the Australian Government has committed $50 million towards the
Sydney to Canberra Rail Corridor Upgrade, to be delivered by the NSW
Government. The project will deliver upgrades to the rail corridor and develop a
business case for longer-term initiatives. With further investment from the NSW and
ACT Governments, there is up to $100m available for this important project.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $4.6 million to
support the delivery of one existing project.

Molonglo Parkway Drive Connector - Planning
This project will deliver a corridor plan for the Molonglo Parkway Drive Connector
and complete detailed designs for key sections of the proposed new road. The
Australian Government has committed an additional $4.6 million towards the project
to enable the completion of detailed design. The total Australian Government
commitment towards the project is now $5 million.
