The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed over $6 million
to new and existing projects in the
Infrastructure Investment Program (IIP),
supporting the delivery and progression of
important upgrades within Tasmania.

Tasmania

This means that as at the 2026-27 Budget,
the Government’s total commitment to
Tasmania under the IIP, over the next
10 years, is $2.2 billion, including
$1.8 billion towards major road and rail
projects and nearly $400 million for smaller
projects and programs such as the Roads
to Recovery, Safer Local Roads and
Infrastructure, and Black Spot programs.
The Government recognises that
infrastructure delivery continues to be
affected by uncertain global conditions,
including market volatility, supply chain
pressures and higher construction costs.
We will continue working with Tasmania to
deliver projects that will benefit Australians
into the future.

New Projects
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $4 million to one
new project in Tasmania.

East Derwent Highway - Bowen Bridge to Bridgewater Improvements Planning
The Australian Government has committed $4 million to the project, which will
undertake planning for upgrades at the East Derwent Highway and Goodwood Road
intersection at the Bowen Bridge, as well as public and active transport
improvements along the highway. Key elements include planning for the future delivery of
intersection, bus stop, pedestrian facilities and active transport infrastructure upgrades.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $2.4 million to
support the delivery of one existing project.

Heavy Vehicle Rest Areas
This project is delivering upgrades to an existing weighbridge site on the Bass
Highway near Howth and constructing a new heavy vehicle rest area at the East
Tamar Highway and Bridport Road junction at Bell Bay. These upgrades will enable
improved rest and compliance facilities for heavy vehicle operators along key freight
routes in Tasmania.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $2.4 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures resulting from construction material increases, the
inclusion of toilet facilities at the sites in response to industry requirements, contaminated soil at the
original East Tamar site, and the associated infrastructure required to supply power and water to
each site.
The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $6.4 million.
