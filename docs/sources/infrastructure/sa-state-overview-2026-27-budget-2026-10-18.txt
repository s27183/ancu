The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed over
$52 million to new and existing projects in
the Infrastructure Investment Program
(IIP), supporting the delivery and
progression of important upgrades within
South Australia.

South Australia

This means that as at the 2026-27 Budget,
the Government’s total commitment to
South Australia under the IIP, over the
next 10 years, is $8.1 billion, including $7.1
billion towards major road and rail projects
and $1.0 billion for smaller projects and
programs such as the Roads to Recovery,
Safer Local Roads and Infrastructure, and
Black Spot programs.
The Government recognises that
infrastructure delivery continues to be
affected by uncertain global conditions,
including market volatility, supply chain
pressures and higher construction costs.
We will continue working with South
Australia to deliver projects that will benefit
Australians into the future.

New Projects
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $7.5 million to a new
project in South Australia.

AUKUS Planning Study and Business Case
The Australian Government has committed $7.5 million to the project, which will
undertake planning for key transport infrastructure upgrades that will improve
commuter and freight access to the Lefevre Peninsula in North-West Adelaide,
including to the AUKUS Defence precinct. Key elements include options analysis,
reference design development, cost estimation, and business case preparation.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2025–26 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $45 million to
support the delivery of existing projects.

South Eastern Freeway Upgrade
This project is delivering essential repairs and safety works to the Heysen Tunnels,
and introducing managed motorway technology to the South Eastern Freeway. This
will improve road safety and reduce delays caused by traffic incidents.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $45 million towards the
project. This additional funding will be used to undertake essential repairs of crib walls
at several locations along the South Eastern Freeway and install variable message signage to inform
road users of incidents.
The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $265 million.
