The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed $556 million to
new projects and $334.2 million to existing
projects in the Infrastructure Investment
Program (IIP), supporting the delivery and
progression of important upgrades within
Western Australia.

Australia to deliver projects that will benefit
Australians into the future.

New Projects
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $556 million to new
projects in Western Australia.

Westport - Anketell Road Upgrades - Stage 1A and 1B
The Australian Government has committed $552 million to the project, which will
upgrade Anketell Road from Leath Road to Abercrombie Road to four lanes,
including a grade separation at Rockingham Road, associated road/road grade
separation structures, intersection upgrades and tie-ins. The project will support the
proposed new Westport container port terminal and deliver increased road capacity, improved freight
efficiency, reduced travel times, and increased safety for all road users.

Great Northern Highway Improvements – Broome to Kununurra
The Australian Government has committed $4 million to the project, which will
deliver detailed planning for upgrades to improve road safety, transport connectivity
and network resilience along the Great Northern Highway between Broome and
Kununurra.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2025–26 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $334.2 million to
support the delivery of existing projects.

METRONET: Thornlie-Cockburn Link
This project delivered a 17.5 kilometre extension of the passenger rail line from
Thornlie to Cockburn Central, including new stations at Ranford Road and Nicholson
Road. This enabled improved public transport connectivity and access between
communities in Perth’s south-eastern corridor and the broader rail network.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $152.3 million towards the
project. This additional funding addresses cost pressures arising from increases in staffing, overhead
and material costs, a longer than anticipated design phase, increased material quantities to meet
technical specification, and higher procurement costs following the re-tendering of critical rail
packages. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $912.3 million.

METRONET: Byford Extension
This project delivered an approximately 8 kilometre extension of the Armadale
passenger rail line south to Byford, including new stations at Armadale and Byford. It
enabled improved public transport connectivity and access for communities in
Perth’s south-eastern growth corridor, supporting access to employment and
services across the metropolitan rail network.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $60.8 million towards the project. This
additional funding addresses ongoing cost pressures arising from enabling works for site conditions,
design changes, increased labour and material costs, and additional resourcing requirements. The
total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $846 million.

METRONET: Midland Station Project
This project delivered construction of a new Midland Station, including associated
bus facilities and parking, and related changes to rail and road interfaces in the
Midland area. This enabled improved integration between rail, bus and active
transport, support safer movement through the precinct, and enhance access to the
metropolitan rail network for passengers in Perth’s eastern suburbs.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $52.7 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from escalation in labour and material costs,
higher than expected tender outcomes, refined design requirements, and contaminated materials
impacting civil works. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now
$252.3 million.

METRONET: Yanchep Rail Extension
This project delivered a 14.5 kilometre extension of the Joondalup passenger rail
line from Butler to Yanchep, including new stations at Yanchep, Alkimos and
Eglinton. This enabled improved public transport connectivity and access for
communities in Perth’s northern growth corridor, supporting travel to employment,
services and the wider metropolitan rail network.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $43.6 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from an extended design phase and significant
escalation in material costs. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now
$847.6 million.

METRONET: Morley-Ellenbrook Line
This project delivered a new passenger rail line between Ellenbrook and the Midland
Line near Bayswater, including six new stations at Bayswater, Morley, Noranda,
Malaga, Whiteman Park and Ellenbrook. This enabled improved public transport
connectivity for Perth’s north‑eastern growth areas, supporting access to
employment, services and the wider metropolitan rail network.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $16.3 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from higher than expected material costs,
additional scope requirements identified during construction, further design works, and increased
costs associated with the re‑award of the primary contractor. The total Australian Government
commitment towards the project is now $1.131 billion.

Flynn Drive Upgrade, Neerabup
This project represents stage one of the broader Flynn Drive upgrades and will
upgrade approximately 3 kilometres between Wanneroo Road and Pinjar Road.
This will enable a dual carriageway connection along the full extent of Flynn Drive,
improving connectivity and access along the Neerabup Strategic Link and supporting
safer movement for vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists in Perth’s northern growth area.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $1.3 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from rising contractor rates, extensive utility
relocations, land acquisition requirements and environmental compliance, compounded by broader
market conditions. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now
$3.8 million

Neerabup Strategic Link – Flynn Drive Upgrade
This project represents stage two of the broader Flynn Drive upgrades and will
upgrade approximately 2 kilometres between Pinjar Road and Old Yanchep Road.
This will enable a dual carriageway connection along the full extent of Flynn Drive,
improving connectivity and access along the Neerabup Strategic Link and supporting
safer movement for vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists in Perth’s northern growth area.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $7.2 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from rising contractor rates, extensive utility
relocations, land acquisition requirements and environmental compliance, compounded by broader
market conditions. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now
$22.2 million.
