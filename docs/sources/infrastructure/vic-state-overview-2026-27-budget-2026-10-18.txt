The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed nearly
$130 million to new projects and
$4.2 billion to existing projects in the
Infrastructure Investment Program (IIP),
supporting the delivery and progression of
important upgrades within Victoria.

Victoria

the Government’s total commitment to
Victoria under the IIP, over the next
10 years, is $21.5 billion, including
$19.1 billion towards major road and rail

deliver projects that will benefit Australians
into the future.

New Projects
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $127.4 million to
three new projects in Victoria.

Melton Line Electrification - Development
The Australian Government has committed $76.4 million to the project, which will
deliver development works to support future electrification of the Melton Line. Key
elements include site investigations, design and planning, and long-lead equipment
procurement to inform future electrification.

Western Freeway Upgrade (Melton to Caroline Springs) Preconstruction
The Australian Government has committed $50 million to deliver preconstruction
works on the Western Freeway between Melton and Caroline Springs, progressing
detailed investigations, design development, and targeted land, planning and
environmental studies.

Princes Highway West - Mackinnons Bridge Strengthening over Mount
Emu Creek, Terang
The Australian Government has committed $1 million to the project, which will
upgrade Mackinnons Bridge over Mount Emu Creek, Terang on the Princes
Highway.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2025–26 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $4.2 billion to
support the delivery of 10 existing projects.

Suburban Rail Loop East
This project is delivering the first section of the Suburban Rail Loop through the
construction of twin rail tunnels and six underground stations across east and south
east Melbourne. This will enable improved connectivity between key employment,
education and health precincts, including Cheltenham, Clayton, Monash, Burwood,
Glen Waverley and Box Hill.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $3.8 billion towards the project. This
additional funding will support delivery of the project by increasing the Australian Government’s
funding contribution. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now
$6.0 billion.

Western Highway - Ballarat to Stawell Duplication
This project is delivering staged duplication of sections along the Western Highway
between Ballarat and Stawell in western Victoria. It will improve safety and support
increased capacity, improved connectivity and more realiable journeys along a key
transport corridor linking western Victoria with Ballarat and the broader transport
network.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $372.5 million towards the project to
address cost pressures arising from delays, escalation and redesign requirements associated for
Section 2B (Buangor to Ararat). The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is
now $970.9 million.

Road Blitz
This project is delivering a package of works to increase capacity and improve
efficiency in Melbourne’s suburbs and surrounds.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $37.4 million towards
Road Blitz to deliver the Donnybrook Road Upgrade – Stage 2 project, which will
duplicate Donnybrook Road over the Hume Freeway and improve the active transport
connections over the freeway interchange.The total Australian Government commitment towards the
project is now over $1.037 billion.

Princes Highway East - Lloyd Street (Moe-Glengarry Road) and Waterloo
Road Intersection Upgrade, Moe
This project will deliver upgrades to the intersection of Lloyd Street (Moe-Glengarry
Road) and Waterloo Road in Moe, including associated road-rail interfaces. This will
enable improved traffic movement and safer integration between road and rail
infrastructure at this key junction on the Princes Highway.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $6.9 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from general escalation, higher track
occupation costs and increased signalling works. The total Australian Government commitment
towards the project is now $16.2 million.

Princes Highway East - Princes Highway and Bank Street Intersection
Upgrade, Traralgon
This project is delivering upgrades to the intersection of Princes Highway and Bank
Street in Traralgon, including improved pedestrian facilities. This will enable safer
and more efficient movement for pedestrians and vehicles at this key intersection on
the Princes Highway.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $5.8 million towards the project. This
additional funding will address cost pressures arising from general escalation, higher track
occupation costs and additional cable trenching works. The total Australian Government commitment
towards the project is now $12.3 million.

Princes Highway West - Blue Church Intersection Upgrade
This project will deliver an upgrade to the intersection of the Princes Highwaywith
Timboon-Colac Road and Nalangil Road at Pirron Yallock, west of Colac. This will
enable improved traffic movement and connectivity at this key junction on the
Princes Highway.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $5.2 million towards the
project. This additional funding will address cost pressures arising from market escalation exceeding
the available contingency following consideration of alternative options which would not have
achieved the project’s intended safety outcomes. The total Australian Government commitment
towards the project is now $15.2 million.

Princes Highway Intersection Upgrades - Pakenham to Beaconsfield
This project has delivered four of the six planned upgrades to intersections along the
Princes Highway between Pakenham and Beaconsfield. This has enabled improved
traffic movement and connectivity along this busy section of the Princes Highway
Corridor.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $2.7 million towards the
project. This additional funding will enable the delivery of the sixth intersection at Arena Parade and
the Princes Highway. The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now
$26.3 million.

Fitzsimons Lane and Main Road Corridor, Eltham - Nillumbik Shire
Council
This project has delivered targeted upgrades along the Fitzsimons Lane and Main
Road corridor in Eltham. This has enabled improved traffic movement and
connectivity along a key local transport corridor in the Nillumbik Shire area.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $1.6 million towards the
project. This additional funding will address cost pressures arising from increased construction costs,
enabling delivery of the project’s final scope item, being the installation of pedestrian-operated traffic
signals on Main Road at Eltham railway station.
The total Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $6.6 million.
