The Australian Government is continuing
to invest in infrastructure around Australia,
investing over $120 billion over 10 years in
the infrastructure investment pipeline. Our
infrastructure projects improve productivity,
resilience, safety and liveability for
communities around the country. We are
carefully managing this investment to
ensure that we can continue to deliver
nationally significant and locally important
infrastructure while retaining responsible
financial management.
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the
Government has committed $95 million to
new projects and $300 million to existing
projects in the Infrastructure Investment
Program (IIP), supporting the delivery and
progression of important upgrades within
New South Wales.

New South Wales

This means that as at the 2026-27 Budget,
the Government’s total commitment to
New South Wales under the IIP, over the
next 10 years, is $17.3 billion, including
$13.9 billion towards major road and rail
projects and $3.4 billion for smaller
projects and programs such as the Roads
to Recovery, Safer Local Roads and
Infrastructure, and Black Spot programs.
The Government recognises that
infrastructure delivery continues to be
affected by uncertain global conditions,
including market volatility, supply chain
pressures and higher construction costs.
We will continue working with New South
Wales to deliver projects that will benefit
Australians into the future.

New Projects
Through the 2026–27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $95 million to new
projects in New South Wales.

Sydney to Canberra Rail Corridor Upgrade
The Australian Government has committed $50 million towards the Sydney to
Canberra Rail Corridor Upgrade, to be delivered by the NSW Government. The
project will deliver upgrades to the rail corridor and develop a business case for
longer-term initiatives. With further investment from the NSW and ACT
Governments, there is up to $100m available for this important project.

M1 Safety Improvements
The Australian Government has committed $45 million to the project, which will
deliver new and upgraded road crossovers and install intelligent transport system
signage on the M1 between Wahroonga and Beresfield.

Projects receiving additional funding
Through the 2026-27 Budget, the Australian Government has committed $300 million to
support the delivery of existing projects.

Liverpool to Airport Transit Corridor, Fifteenth Avenue
This project will upgrade Fifteenth Avenue as part of a vital east west public
transport corridor between Liverpool, Bradfield and the Western Sydney Airport.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $200 million towards the
project. This additional funding will allow for the full scope of upgrades to be delivered
on Fifteenth Avenue, extending from Cowpasture Road through to Devonshire Road. The total
Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $700 million.

Elizabeth Drive - Priority Sections Upgrade
This project will deliver upgrades to priority sections of Elizabeth Drive in Western
Sydney. This will enable improved support for freight and commuter traffic, and
support access to the Western Sydney International Airport and Aerotropolis
precincts.
The Australian Government has committed an additional $100 million towards the
project. This additional funding will allow the full scope of upgrades to priority sections to be
delivered, extending from east of Devonshire Road through to Badgerys Creek Road. The total
Australian Government commitment towards the project is now $500 million.
