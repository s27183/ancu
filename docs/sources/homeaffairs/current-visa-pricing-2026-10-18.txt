​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​Current visa pricing table
Use the Visa Prices table to help find the price of your visa.
The table groups visas by what you plan to do in Australia. The categories are:
visit
study
work
live
other
​repealed or closed visas.
For some visas, a lower visa cost may apply if you are an eligible citizen of a Pacific Island country, Timor-Leste or an ASEAN member country and you lodge a valid visa application.
To work out your visa cost, use the Visa Pricing Estimator .
