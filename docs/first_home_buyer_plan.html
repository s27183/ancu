The plan in 90 seconds

Since October 2025, the federal First Home Guarantee has had no income cap and no place cap. With a 5% deposit, the government guarantees the rest and you avoid Lenders Mortgage Insurance. Brisbane / Gold Coast / Sunshine Coast cap is $1.0M; Melbourne / Geelong is $950k.

From 1 May 2025, Queensland gives first home buyers a full stamp duty exemption with no price cap on new builds. Combined with FHG, your $50k can be a 5% deposit on a $1M new build with zero stamp duty and zero LMI.

Catch: you still need ~$19–28k cash on top of the deposit for inspection, conveyancing, insurance, settlement fees, and a 3-month cash reserve. Realistic working figure for the full $1M target is $65–75k accessible at settlement.

Best path

QLD new build up to $1M. Federal FHG removes LMI on 5% deposit. QLD first-home new-home concession removes stamp duty. Most efficient use of your cash.

Safer path

QLD established under $700k. FHG + QLD first home concession both kick in. $35k deposit + ~$3k legals = only ~$38k cash needed. Leaves a real buffer.

What you need beyond the $50k cash

The deposit is just one piece. Here's everything else.

Documents to gather (weeks 0–2)

People to engage

  • Mortgage broker with FHG panel access — free to you, paid by lender. Submits the FHG reservation on your behalf.
  • Conveyancer or solicitor — engage before signing anything. ~$1,500–2,500.
  • Building & pest inspector — $500–700 for established. For new build, replaced by pre-handover inspection.
  • Insurer — building insurance must be in force from contract exchange (QLD) or settlement (VIC). Confirm with conveyancer.

Watch out: Lenders increasingly want to see a post-settlement cash reserve in your name as part of the serviceability assessment. If you'd settle with literally $0 left, some lenders won't approve.

FHG-specific gates to clear

  • Property under the cap ($1M Brisbane/Gold Coast/Sunshine Coast; $950k Melbourne/Geelong)
  • Owner-occupier intent (cannot be investment)
  • First home buyer status confirmed by state revenue office
  • FHG slot reserved by lender — usually a ~14–90 day window to find a property once reserved
  • For QLD new build path: confirm property meets "new home" definition under QRO rules (off-the-plan, never occupied, or substantially renovated)

Ongoing obligations after settlement

Monthly (recurring direct debits)

  • Mortgage P&I to lender — on $1M at 95% LVR (loan ≈ $950k) at ~6.0% over 30 years = roughly $5,700/month. Build in ~$300/month buffer for rate moves.
  • Utilities (electricity, gas, internet) — $300–500/month combined.
  • Optional: contents insurance, income protection, life insurance.

Quarterly

  • Council rates to local council — $400–700/quarter ($1,600–2,800/year)
  • Water access charge + usage — $200–350/quarter
  • Strata / body corporate levies (if apartment/townhouse) — $700–1,500/quarter for typical Brisbane apartment
  • Emergency services / fire levies in some council areas

Annually

  • Building insurance premium — $800–1,500/year. Mandatory while mortgaged.
  • Mortgage statement — review and consider refinancing if uncompetitive.
  • Tax return to ATO — no special FHG handling. If you used FHSS, released amount appears as assessable income with a 30% tax offset.
  • Land tax — usually $0 for principal place of residence in QLD and VIC.

Event-driven (life-of-loan)

  • Maintenance reserve — budget ~1% of property value/year ($10k/year on a $1M home)
  • Refinancing — review every 2–4 years to chase competitive rates
  • Selling or moving out — FHG ends when loan paid off, refinanced, or property sold. No clawback.
  • If you stop owner-occupying (e.g. rent it out), loan may breach FHG conditions. Notify lender first.
The "graduation" event: When your LVR drops below 80% (typically 4–8 years in), the FHG is no longer doing work for you. You can refinance freely to any lender, full market open, no LMI either way. You've become a regular mortgage holder.
